How to use this template:
- Replace the examples and the text in square brackets with your own; add as many scenes as you need.
- Keep headings in the form “Scene N: Title”. Numbers follow the reading order.
- In the “Characters:” (or “Participants:”) line, list names separated by commas, exactly as in the headings of the characters file. Leave the line empty for a scene without characters.
- Day 1 is the starting point you choose. When a scene crosses midnight, give the new day in “End”. A calendar date can replace the day number.
- Time is the time of events in the story world. For a flashback, state the day of its events explicitly; the scene order stays the reading order.
- The age rating is not set in the plan: set it in the story settings; generation does not start without it.
- Check that travel, conversations, waiting and other actions fit the time span. The app keeps these marks in the plan but does not compute durations or check contradictions automatically.
