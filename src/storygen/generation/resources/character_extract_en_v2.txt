You read a book's prose in groups of scenes to build character cards, the cast and the places of every scene.
Rules:
- Use only what is in the text; invent nothing.
- A character is a being that acts or speaks in a scene or matters to the plot; groups of people are not characters.
- A first-person narrator ("I") is a character too: if the narrator's name is given anywhere, use the name, otherwise call the card "Narrator".
- A new card has 1-2 VERBATIM quotes from the text provided (an exact substring, 5-20 words, at most 300 characters) that show this character.
- If a character is already in the catalogue (including under another name, a diminutive, a nickname, a surname, an inflected form), do NOT create a new card: add the new name to the existing one (card_updates). Write names in the nominative form.
- The name (name) and every other name (other_names) are at most 80 characters; at most 10 other names.
- For EVERY scene of the group list the participants: the characters present in the scene.
- For EVERY scene list 1-3 places of the action (locations) in the order the action moves. A place is a concrete place where the action of the scene happens, not one only mentioned, remembered or imagined. Write a nested place as "Wider / Narrower" (for example "Pasture / Hut"), in the nominative form, at most 120 characters. Give a place from the location catalogue by its ID (l1); a narrower place inside a catalogue place as "l8 / Narrower".
- Protagonist candidates: at most 3, the ones the text of the group revolves around, each with a score from 0 to 1.
- Use only the short IDs from the input: scenes s<N>, catalogue cards c<N>, catalogue locations l<N>; invent the keys of new cards (n1, n2...) yourself and repeat them in scenes and protagonist_candidates.
Answer with JSON ONLY:
{"new_cards":[{"key":"n1","name":"...","other_names":["..."],"quotes":["..."]}],
 "card_updates":[{"id":"c1","other_names":["..."]}],
 "scenes":[{"scene":"s3","participants":["c1","n1"],"locations":["l2","New place / Narrower"]}],
 "protagonist_candidates":[{"id":"c1 or n1","score":0.8}]}
