## Why this policy exists

The code licence lets you read, modify and use the code, but it grants no rights to the name or logo (the Trademarks clause of [FSL-1.1-MIT](../../LICENSE)). The name lets writers tell an official build from someone else's. An official build is one distributed by the project author.

## What is covered

- The names **Obscura** and **Obscura Text Writer**.
- The logo: the icon in `src/storygen/web/static/favicon.svg` and the "Obscura" / "Text Writer" wording next to it.
- Names and marks that could be confused with the above.

## What you may do without asking

- Mention the project in articles, reviews, teaching material and discussions.
- Link to the official repository and website.
- State where your project comes from as a fact: "based on Obscura Text Writer" or "a fork of Obscura Text Writer".
- Pass on an unmodified official build with its name and logo, where the code licence allows it.
- Use the name in a local copy for your own needs, even a modified one.

## What needs written permission

- Distributing a modified or self-built copy under the Obscura name or with the logo. Rename such a copy and remove the logo from its interface, icons and description.
- Presenting your build, service, pack or extension as official or endorsed by the project author.
- Using the name or logo in the name of a product, domain, account or company.
- Altering the logo or combining it with other marks.

## Permissions and questions

To ask for permission or ask a question, open an issue in the [project repository](https://github.com/ysilvestrov/obscura-text-writer/issues). This policy may change; the current revision is always in the repository.
