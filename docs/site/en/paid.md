# Paid version

A paid version of Obscura Text Writer is in preparation. It gives what the source-code version gives, as an ordinary Microsoft Store installation:

- **long prose:** books with parts and arcs and the Book structure panel — see [Parts and arcs](book-structure.md);
- **book series:** the next book continues the previous one with a snapshot of memory and characters — see [Book series](series.md);
- **explicit level 2** of sexual content, which the Microsoft Store build does not have — see [Age rating](content-rating.md).

There is no price or release date yet.

## Trial in the Microsoft Store build

The Microsoft Store build lets you try long prose once on your own text:

- **what the trial gives:** one book with parts and arcs, or one series of two books and a world bible. There is one trial per works folder, the choice cannot be undone, and parts and arcs stay in those books for good;
- **how to turn it on:** in the settings of a flat book, press **Try parts and arcs on this book**. The message above a book with parts made in another build has the same button. The **Create the trial series** button on the Series page creates a trial series, and an existing series with at most two books becomes the trial series with **Make this the trial series**. Another way is the [manuscript import](manuscript-import.md) with the **Import with parts as the trial book** checkbox. The application asks for confirmation first;
- **what stays paid:** a third book of the trial series, a second series, a second book with parts. The rating limit of the Microsoft Store build is the same in the trial — see [Age rating](content-rating.md);
- **the trash does not return the trial:** if you delete the trial book or series, you cannot choose another one. A book that leaves the trial series keeps its parts and arcs, but its place in the series is not freed.
